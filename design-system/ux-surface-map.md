## P01 Admin/Ops Includes
- Tenant creation form
- Manual client creation form
- Client-tenant assignment
- Coach assignment
- Provisioning status tracker
- Invite management
- Audit log view (read-only)

## P01 Admin/Ops Excludes
- Protocol assignment
- Journey assignment with content
- Client-facing views
- Payment/commerce
